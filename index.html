Healthy oceans need their apex predators.

Every year, an estimated 73 million sharks are killed for their fins. Finwell Ocean Trust works with scientists, coastal communities and lawmakers to end shark finning and rebuild the fisheries that depend on balanced marine ecosystems.

What we do

Our programs

Science, policy and people — three approaches that only work together.

Research

Pelagic Tracking Initiative

Satellite and acoustic tagging of hammerhead, silky and whale sharks to map migration corridors and identify critical nursery habitat between Mexico and the Galápagos.

Policy

Fin Free Pacific

Campaigning for enforceable fins-naturally-attached laws, port inspections and transparent catch reporting across the Eastern Pacific's major landing ports.

Communities

Coastal Livelihoods

Partnering with 60+ fishing cooperatives in Mexico, Ecuador and Fiji to build income from eco-tourism, selective gear and certified sustainable catch.
