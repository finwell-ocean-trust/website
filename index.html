Est. 2011 · Monterey, California

Healthy oceans need their apex predators.

Every year, an estimated 73 million sharks are killed for their fins. Finwell Ocean Trust works with scientists, coastal communities and lawmakers to end shark finning and rebuild the fisheries that depend on balanced marine ecosystems.
